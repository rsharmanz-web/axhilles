How to run the workshop

Spreadsheet to think · board to see · finance department, any industry
← Status board

One big job: run finance so the business can operate, stay solvent, and stand behind the numbers. The spine is the sequential jobs a finance team is hired to do. The spreadsheet is where the room thinks. The board is that sheet, lit up.

In the room
01

Open the sheet

Do
tasks.xlsx in Excel or Google Sheets, on a shared screen. One row is one task. This is the thinking surface — same as the Baker Tilly audit workshop. Save as CSV when you want it on the board.
02

Line by line

Do
Rapid-fire down a job. For each task: is this a real job here, what lens, does it take hours / days / weeks, then current / ceiling (1–3). Do not try to automate the whole job in one hit. Ceiling can be 2.
03

Rewrite the examples

Do
Rewrite the examples cell for this business. Lender, retailer, wholesaler, consulting are already in the straw man so the room has something to argue with, not a blank page.
04

Light the board

Do
Load the sheet into the HTML board. Status lamps are the Innovation / steering view. Download the sheet at the end so the room takes the thinking with them.

The gap between current and ceiling is what you might actually build. Hours / days / weeks tells you whether the job is worth the room’s time. A locked judgement task can still have a simple support pack sitting underneath it.

The three lenses
LensMeaningBar the build has to clear
CoordinationPulling data together so a human can decide, or moving work through a process.Documentation only. If it fails, you are slow, not wrong to a standard.
EvidenceProof someone else will rely on — IRD, auditor, bank, board.Reproducible. Named reviewer. Completeness you can show.
JudgementA human has to make a call.The conclusion stays human. You may automate the ammunition, never the finding.
After the room — the same five gates as the audit board

Every task that gets a status other than unmapped should move through this, so the next hundred ideas do not get decided case by case.

Diamond 1 · Diverge
01

Discovery

Purpose
Walk the spine with the people who do the work. Find the recurring pain. Skip jobs this business does not have.
Exit gate
Is there a real, recurring finance-team pain here, and could a tool plausibly help?
Diamond 1 · Converge
02

Exploration

Purpose
One task, a lens, hours / days / weeks, simple / medium / complex, and current vs ceiling.
Exit gate
Lens assigned, scores agreed, and nobody is trying to automate the whole job.
Diamond 2 · Diverge
03

Proof of concept

Purpose
The smallest thing that tests the real risk — on last month’s actuals, not a demo file.
Exit gate
Did it save real time or produce a pack someone will sign, on real numbers?
Diamond 2 · Converge
04

Field testing

Purpose
Run it through a live close with more than one user, under the FC or CFO.
Exit gate
Measured time saved or error rate, signed off by the person who owns the number.
Operate
05

Live

Purpose
Rolled into the close. Documented, owned, revisited when the stack or the standard changes.
Exit gate
Still fit at the next close. If it needs the person who built it, it is not live.
