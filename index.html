02 — About

Most “AI for business” pitches now come from people who discovered AI in 2023. Rahul Sharma was implementing it inside a global product team before it was a pitch anyone was making.

Over a 17-year career spanning strategy, design and product leadership — including senior roles at Sky TV, Les Mills International and ANZ Bank — he led Xero’s AI design research, taking its AI feature set from first prototype to global rollout, and pioneered the way Xero’s global design team adopted these tools.

He’ll also tell you what didn’t work: the pilots that were quietly shelved, the tools nobody kept using. That track record — not just the wins — is what he’s built Axhilles around.

Axhilles is the collective he’s assembled from it: senior AI practitioners who’ve done this at scale, brought in case by case around the size and shape of your engagement. The result is enterprise-grade thinking, built to help businesses operate like they’ve got a team twice their size.
