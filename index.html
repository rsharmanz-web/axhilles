02 — About

Axhilles is an innovation collective helping service businesses systematically apply AI in complex environments. Founder Rahul Sharma draws on a 17-year career spanning research, strategy, design and product leadership for Sky Broadband, Les Mills International and ANZ Bank. Since 2024, he’s led Xero’s AI Design Research team, scaling its AI features across the company’s product footprint.

Drawing on that experience, and a network of senior AI practitioners, Axhilles can share what worked as readily as what didn’t — the pilots that took off and the ones quietly shelved, the tools that stuck and the ones switched off. That track record is what Axhilles is built on. The result is enterprise-grade thinking, built to help businesses operate like they’ve got a team twice their size.
