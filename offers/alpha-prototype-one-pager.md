## The promise

Most AI projects die in a deck, or sprawl into a build nobody can test. An Alpha Prototype does neither. In about two weeks we take one real workflow, job, or task and build a solution you can put in front of users. Internal or external. Alongside the thing itself, you leave with a repeatable framework for product development so the next idea doesn't start from scratch.

No backend. No backend integration. The point is learning fast, not wiring the stack.

---

## Who it's for

Teams that already know the painful job they want to fix. Often that job came straight off a Baseline to Roadmap map. Sometimes a partner spotted it and Discovery scoped it clean.

Not for you if you need production backend work, a firm-wide rollout, or you're still exploring AI with nothing concrete to test.

---

## How it works (~2 weeks)

We run one full pass of a product-development loop on a single, bounded problem. You get the prototype and the playbook.

1. Discovery: understand the workflow, job, or task and who it serves
2. Define scope / PRD: lock what "good" looks like (template provided)
3. Concept development: shape and build the alpha
4. User testing: put it in front of real users, internal or external
5. One round of refinement: fix what the testing showed
6. Go / no-go decision: keep, iterate, or stop with eyes open

**Explicitly out of scope**
- Backend build
- Backend integration

**Required input**
- Access to the people who own the workflow
- Users for testing (internal or external)
- Feedback through discovery, concept, and refinement

That's it.
